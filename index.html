Where she works

Sammamish and Eastside neighborhoods

Most of Ericka's business is on the Sammamish plateau, with steady sales across the communities around it.

Sammamish

Sahalee Estates, Timberline, Heritage Hills and the Eastlake school neighborhoods. Her core market.

More
  • Neighborhoods: Sahalee Estates and Sahalee Country Club, Timberline Ridge, Heritage Hills, Pine Lake, Beaver Lake, Klahanie and the Plateau.
  • Schools: The north plateau feeds Lake Washington School District (Eastlake High School); the south plateau is in Issaquah School District (Skyline High School).
  • Lifestyle: Wooded lots, lake parks and 27 holes at Sahalee Country Club, with Redmond and Bellevue jobs a short drive away.

Redmond

Acreage estates on Union Hill and Novelty Hill, including her highest recorded sale.

More
  • Neighborhoods: Union Hill, Novelty Hill, Redmond Ridge, Education Hill and Downtown Redmond.
  • Schools: Lake Washington School District, including Redmond High School.
  • Lifestyle: Acreage and equestrian properties east of town, Marymoor Park and trails, Microsoft’s campus, and light rail into downtown Redmond.

Bellevue

Lake Hills and Crossroads homes close to downtown Bellevue and the tech campuses.

More
  • Neighborhoods: Lake Hills, Crossroads, Somerset, Bridle Trails and Woodridge.
  • Schools: Bellevue School District, including Interlake, Sammamish and Bellevue High Schools.
  • Lifestyle: Downtown Bellevue shopping and dining, the East Link light rail line, and quick access to I-90 and SR 520.

Issaquah

Wooded multi-acre properties between Cougar Mountain and the Issaquah Alps.

More
  • Neighborhoods: Issaquah Highlands, Talus, Squak Mountain, Cougar Mountain and Montreux.
  • Schools: Issaquah School District, including Issaquah and Skyline High Schools.
  • Lifestyle: Trailheads into Tiger, Squak and Cougar Mountains, Lake Sammamish State Park, historic Gilman Village and an easy I-90 commute.

Kirkland

Lake Washington waterfront living, walkable downtown and close-in Eastside neighborhoods.

More
  • Neighborhoods: Downtown and Moss Bay, Houghton, Juanita, Rose Hill, Bridle Trails and Totem Lake.
  • Schools: Lake Washington School District, including Lake Washington and Juanita High Schools.
  • Lifestyle: Waterfront parks and marinas, a lively downtown on the lake, and tech campuses close by.
