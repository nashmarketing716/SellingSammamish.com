Living in Sahalee Estates

Sahalee Country Club is one of the most celebrated private clubs in the Pacific Northwest. Its 27 holes have hosted the 1998 PGA Championship, the 2010 U.S. Senior Open and the 2016 KPMG Women's PGA Championship.

Homes sit on wooded lots along Sahalee Way and the streets that wind around the course, with quick access to Redmond, Bellevue and the Microsoft campus. Lake Sammamish, Pine Lake and the shops at Sammamish Highlands are a short drive away.

Sahalee Estates home market
