Living in Timberline

Timberline, including the Timberline Ridge area with views toward Lake Sammamish, is known for its mature evergreens, larger lots and quiet cul-de-sacs. It offers a quick commute north to Redmond and the Microsoft campus, and easy access to Sammamish parks and trails.

Most north-plateau homes are in the Lake Washington School District. Confirm the boundary for any specific address before you buy.

Timberline home market

Timberline homes are mostly single-family houses, many built in the 1980s and 1990s and updated since. Updated kitchens, outdoor living space and lot privacy drive the biggest price differences.
